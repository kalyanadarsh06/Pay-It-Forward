Every fee sweep, every per-holder split, and every outbound donation is computed by an agent running in a VM we do not have shell access to during a sweep. The VM screen is streamed continuously; recordings are kept and linked from Proofs.

Money. Creator fees are income to the developers. We hold back a tax reserve on every sweep (currently 20%, to be confirmed by our accountant and published here) and route the remainder to the GoFundMe campaigns nominated by holders, pro-rata to how much $PIF each wallet held and for how long during the sweep window. Donations to personal GoFundMe campaigns are gifts and are generally not tax-deductible for anyone. We are not a charity and do not take a cut beyond the tax reserve. Rate changes are announced on X before they take effect and logged below.

Verify. Fee sweep tx hashes are on the explorer. Donation confirmations are GoFundMe's own receipt emails, shown unedited in each recording. If a number on this page disagrees with the chain or with GoFundMe, the chain and GoFundMe are right and we will fix the page. Report mistakes to ledger@payitforward.fund or @payitforward.
