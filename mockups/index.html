How a hold becomes a donation

worked example · the same numbers the agent is computing above

Every 15 minutes the agent sweeps the creator fees $PIF earned on Robinhood Chain. It snapshots every wallet holding at least half a percent, once a minute, and weights each wallet by how much it held and for how many of those minutes.

Your weight is your share of the sweep. A fixed tax reserve comes off the top, because the fees are taxable income to us before they ever reach a campaign. What is left goes to the GoFundMe campaign you nominated when you bought in. The agent fills in the donation form itself, on camera, and files the receipt.

Hold for only part of a window and your weight is prorated. Sell everything and your pending share goes to the pool, which is split across every other campaign in the next sweep. Nothing is ever held back for us beyond the reserve.

If you can’t see it being done, assume it wasn’t.

The agent runs in a VM with one job. It reads the chain, does the arithmetic out loud, opens GoFundMe, pays, and files the receipt. Humans do not touch the keyboard during a sweep. If the stream drops, sweeps pause until it is back.

Pay It Forward ($PIF) · Robinhood Chain · contract 0x5c1e9a4f7b2d8e3c6a0f1b9d4e7c2a8f5b3d6e1c · deployed block 4,018,772 · creator-fee wallet 0x8f3a…c21e. Every fee sweep, every per-holder split, and every outbound donation is computed by an agent running in a VM we do not have shell access to during a sweep. The VM screen is streamed continuously; recordings are kept and linked from Proofs.

Money. Creator fees are income to the developers. We hold back a tax reserve on every sweep (currently 20%, to be confirmed by our accountant and published here) and route the remainder to the GoFundMe campaigns nominated by holders, pro-rata to how much $PIF each wallet held and for how long during the sweep window. Donations to personal GoFundMe campaigns are gifts and are generally not tax-deductible for anyone. We are not a charity and do not take a cut beyond the tax reserve. Rate changes are announced on X before they take effect and logged below.

Verify. Fee sweep tx hashes are on the explorer. Donation confirmations are GoFundMe's own receipt emails, shown unedited in each recording. If a number on this page disagrees with the chain or with GoFundMe, the chain and GoFundMe are right and we will fix the page. Report mistakes to ledger@payitforward.fund or @payitforward.
