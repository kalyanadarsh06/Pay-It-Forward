# Surface brief: prototype/index.html (guided first visit + ledger)

Scope: the first-visit flow hosted by Earthy, and the ledger it lands on (all-time leaderboard, live ledger, proofs gallery, totals, token address). Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: Robinhood Chain traders arriving from GMGN-style feeds, plus the skeptics they forward the link to. Job: decide in under a minute whether the donations are real. Action: copy the token address and open the launchpad. Proof: the latest donation recording with the card redacted and the GoFundMe receipt shown; fee tx hashes; sourced world-state figures. Constraints: founder-pinned references (prototype/ref/*.png), Obsidian/Notion baseline, more color, Earthy with hands; skippable; never shown twice; reduced-motion safe; no invented commercial claims; all sweep figures are sample data until the agent feed exists.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A living field and a calm document. The left of the screen is where the agent lives: a dark, slowly moving luminous field where Earthy stands and speaks. The right is the record: a pale, hairline-structured document in the Notion/Obsidian register where every claim sits as a line item. The split itself is the argument: warmth on one side, receipts on the other. It refuses the single-column memecoin landing page and the neon terminal alike.

OWN-WORLD: Field: near-black violet ground with three drifting blurred color masses (violet, magenta, coral) that never stop moving, bounded to the field. Document: warm pale gray paper, near-black ink, two gray tiers, one hairline. Accents, each with one job: lime spark (the brand mark and live state), leaf green (settled money), amber (Earthy and his hands), violet (links and hover). Display: Instrument Serif at high contrast for headings and every large number. Body and labels: Hanken Grotesk; labels are tracked uppercase at 11.5px+. Hashes and addresses only in Martian Mono. Primary actions are large outlined rectangles with a serif label left and an arrow right that fill to ink on hover. No cards with shadows; structure is hairlines and whitespace. Earthy: an amber orb with eyes, brows, cheeks, a morphing mouth and two round hands on articulated groups; gestures per step: wave, hands to cheeks, point, count, raise, cheer, present; idle breath and blink.

STORY: Earthy says hello and waves; shows the world as it is (hands to cheeks); points at the last donation recording and its receipt; counts out where that money came from; raises a hand for the last five minutes' top donor; cheers the totals; presents the token address with both hands. The visitor leaves believing "they film it, they keep receipts, the holders pick where it goes," and has the address copied.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a 40/60 split. Left field full height: wordmark top-left in serif, Earthy (about 180px) centered with his spoken line beneath in pale type, live status and sweep metadata in tracked caps at the bottom. Right document: tracked-caps nav top-right with the lime spark; a six-segment hairline progress bar; the step heading in serif at display size; the step's content; the large outlined action at the bottom of the document with the next step's name and an arrow; "Skip" quiet beside it. Signature interaction: Earthy's gestures are synchronized with the content arriving: figures and rows rise 8px and fade in as his hand moves, and the field's color masses brighten a step when a donation is shown. Reduced motion: field stills, Earthy holds the gesture's end pose, content crossfades.

FORM: Founder-pinned direction from the supplied references (beats seed 8304a059's clay assignment; the roll's challengers and raises are retired). Build path: code-led.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
