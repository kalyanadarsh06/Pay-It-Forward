          How the money moves. Every minutes the agent sweeps the creator fees $PIF earned, weights every wallet holding at least of supply by how much it held and for how long, holds back a reserve for the tax owed on that income, and donates each wallet’s share to the GoFundMe campaign that wallet nominated. The reserve rate is confirmed by our accountant and published here before it changes.

          What we are not. We are not a charity. Gifts to personal GoFundMe campaigns are generally not tax-deductible for anyone, including us. We don’t take a cut beyond the reserve. If a number here disagrees with the chain or with GoFundMe, they are right and we fix the page.
