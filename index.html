        QUESTIONS

        Who decides where the money goes?

        Holders. Each holder backs one cause, by posting a thesis on FOMO or by connecting a wallet here. Every payout is split between causes by how much $PIF their backers hold and how long they've held it. To keep it fair, no single cause can take more than a fixed maximum of any payout.

        What causes can I pick?

        Any cause with an online donation page: a charity's donate page, a GoFundMe, a school or hospital fund. If our agents can pay it online, it counts.

        How does the money reach my cause?

        All fees collect on the $PIF virtual card. On each payout, our agents (software that uses a web browser the way a person does) open the cause's donation page, pay from the card and save the receipt.

        Can I watch a payment happen?

        Yes. Every payout has a Watch button. While a payout is running you see the agent's browser live as it opens the donation page, enters the amount, pays and saves the receipt. Finished payouts keep their recording.

        Can viewers see the card number?

        No. Card details are hidden in every live view and recording.

        Where does the other 20% go?

        We keep 20% of fees to cover taxes. The 80/20 split never changes.

        How do I know payouts are real?

        Every payout in the log has a recording of the agent making it and the receipt from the cause, so anyone can check that the money in matches the money out.
